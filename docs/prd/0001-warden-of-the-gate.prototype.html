Warden of the Gate / throwaway logic prototype

The factory, one decision at a time.

Can a bug or approved feature move through the eight-task loop without stealing a claim, losing a deadline, repeating an accepted review, or merging stale work? Explore the rules before building the real factory.

Offline simulation only. No GitHub, model, credentials, network requests or saved data. Refreshing wipes everything. Buttons simulate outcomes; they do not prove model quality, SDK enforcement, durable storage or execution isolation.
