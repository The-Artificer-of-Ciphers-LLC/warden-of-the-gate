## P4 groundwork built offline (branch `p4/merge-gates-2026-11-18`)

- **Merge eligibility** (`packages/warden/src/merge/gates.ts`, pure): blocker always gates; major/minor/nit follow config; risk assessed independently (missing risk is uncertain); high or uncertain risk, workflow/access/maintainer/standing-order paths and `wontfix` need a maintainer; origin comes from the verified author login, never a label; automation breaking majors and migrations cannot bypass escalation; independent GitHub approvals exclude the author, the App and factory identities (a second model session is not an identity); draft, stop and hold block.
- **Merge delivery through the writer**: new `review-record` (AI origin, so shadow mode records nothing and nothing can merge before live) and `merge-request` intents; the merge effect is bound to the head the requester saw, GitHub must confirm before `mark-merged` updates the ledger, stale heads are clean rejections without retry, duplicates and lost acknowledgements produce one merge. `warden.yml` gained `merge.{automationLogins,factoryLogins,requiredApprovals}`.
- **Rework accounting**: three completed cycles persisted on the ledger (progress or not), separate launch-retry budget, success returns to Review, unresolved after the last cycle escalates; `carry-accounting` gives a replacement PR the replaced PR's counts (read by the writer).
- **Critical escape**: pauses the item, preserves evidence in the ledger, alerts maintainers; only notes and a source-verified `/warden resume-scope` are accepted while paused.
- **Factory PR body** (scope, approach, evidence, AI attribution, Merge Danger door type, never claims review or merge) and **takeover rules** (factory branches only, credit ported contributors, execution surfaces never run).

**Not built in P4:** the factory Implement/Rework execution itself (write-capable OpenCode session in an isolated worktree, fail-before/pass-after evidence from the trusted executor), takeover execution, branch-protection introspection (required reviews/checks are taken from config and the PR's approvals), linked-issue resolution after confirming merged scope, remediation proposals after an escape, and the dependency/CVE advisory verification (the flag is an input, not verified here).
